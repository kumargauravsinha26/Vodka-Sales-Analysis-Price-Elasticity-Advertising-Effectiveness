🥂 Vodka Sales Analysis – Price Elasticity & Advertising Effectiveness

This project applies multiple linear regression modeling to analyze sales performance across vodka brands and estimate price elasticity and advertising effectiveness.
Using SAS, the analysis identifies how changes in price, advertising spend, and brand tier influence sales volume and profitability.

📊 Key Highlights

-Built regression models on 263 observations with an Adjusted R² of 0.71, indicating strong model fit.

-Estimated price elasticity = −0.85, revealing inelastic demand and opportunities for moderate price increases.

-Determined that broadcast and outdoor advertising are the most effective media drivers for sales growth.

-Delivered actionable insights for optimizing marketing mix and brand tier strategy for Svedka and competing vodka brands.

🧠 Skills & Tools

-Tools: SAS, Excel

-Techniques: Multiple Linear Regression, Dummy Variable Encoding, Elasticity Estimation, Model Diagnostics

-Business Focus: Marketing Mix Optimization, Pricing Strategy, Advertising ROI
